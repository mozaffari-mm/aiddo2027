AIDDO 2027 — CALL FOR PAPERS

AI for Database Schema Design and Optimization
Workshop at the 43rd IEEE International Conference on Data Engineering (ICDE 2027)
Copenhagen, Denmark

AIDDO brings together researchers and practitioners interested in how AI and machine learning can support database design, optimization, adaptation, and the evolution of data-intensive systems.

TOPICS OF INTEREST

Topics include, but are not limited to:

- AI-assisted conceptual, logical, and physical database design
- Schema generation and schema recommendation
- AI for schema evolution, matching, integration, migration, and transformation
- Learned cost, cardinality, and performance models
- AI-guided design-space exploration and reinforcement learning
- Workload-aware and query-driven database optimization
- Self-tuning, autonomous, online, and proactive database systems
- AI-assisted indexing, partitioning, clustering, materialized views, storage layouts, and data placement
- LLMs and generative AI for database modeling, schema reasoning, design assistance, and query-aware design
- NoSQL, multi-model, temporal, graph, vector, and heterogeneous data systems
- Store-independent and cross-model database design
- Human-in-the-loop and explainable AI-assisted database design
- Benchmarks, empirical studies, industrial experiences, tools, demonstrations, and reproducibility

PLANNED SUBMISSION CATEGORIES

- Full research papers: up to 8 pages plus references
- Short papers: up to 4 pages plus references
- Extended abstracts: up to 2 pages plus references

IMPORTANT DATES

- Paper submission: February 3, 2027
- Author notification: February 24, 2027
- Camera-ready: March 10, 2027
- Workshop date: to be announced

Final formatting, submission-system, and publication instructions will follow the ICDE 2027 workshop proceedings requirements.
